  - Automatic train arrival movewment features: 
    - At the time of train arrival timing, train should be shown at the entry section and wait for route locking, platform locking and assigning the platform number to the train.
    - System trigger that the train is arrived at the outer section/s of the station.
    - System then look for the available platform for the train, once the available platform is found, assign the found platform to the train. 
    - System will Then select the best possible available route (set of sections) which is going till that assigned platform for the train. 
    - System will select the route and lock the route and platform for the train (route (set of sections) selecting and locking should be highlighted in the VDU with the orange color).
    - System will llook for the signals and protected sections which is going to be crossed by the train through the locked/occoupied route.
    - System will ensure that route is locked/occoupied, then system will change the aspects for all signals to yellow/green which is going to be crossed by the train in that route.
    - System will increase the speed gradgually and move the train from the entry section to the assigned platform, through the assigned route smoothly.
    - System will monitor that While moving the train, if the signal aspect is yellow then train speed is 1-30 kmph, if the signal aspect is green then train speed is 30-60 kmph.
    - System will monitor the signals when the train is moving and when the train is at the signal's protected section, system will change the aspect of the signal to red (It will indicate that train is crossed the protected section and signal is now crossed).
    - As the train is moving one-by-one section, system will monitor and unlocked the crossed sections from the locked/occoupied route.
    - System will monitor the train's position and when train reaches to the assigned platform, system will be unlocked the assigned route. (Assigned Platform to the train should be still assigned as the train is on the platform section, platform section still be occoupied ).
    - Showing that the train is standing on the platform.
    - System will monotior the arrived train's wait_time (in seconds).
    - As the train's wait_time is over, the train color is changed to the light blue color.
  
  - Automatic train departure process:
    - Departure process will be started, for the departure, first check the direction of the train, then look for the possible available (unoccoupied) route from the POSSIBLE_ROUTES in the routes.py file. 
    - Select the route for that train from which platform is standing to the from where that train should exit section (Not a signle section should nbe occoupied by other trains).
    - System will check that the selected route for that train is not occoupied by other trains, if no route is available, then the train will be waiting on the platform for the route to be free.
    - If the route is available, then lock the route for that train, 
    - Once the route is locked the other train cannot lock the same route.
    - System will analyse the signals which are going to be crossed by the train through the locked route, while departuring.
    - System will now change the signals through the locked route to yellow/green aspect, so that train should be able to depart and move.
    - System will now move the train from the platform to the exit section, through the locked route smoothly animation.
    - gradgually increase the speed of the train,
    - As the train is moving one-by-one section, system will monitor and unlocked the crossed sections from the locked/occoupied route.
    - System will monitor the train's position and as the train passes the signals, system will change the aspect of the signals to red, and unlock the previous section.

  - This all automation process for every train which is arriving from any entry section and every departure sections
